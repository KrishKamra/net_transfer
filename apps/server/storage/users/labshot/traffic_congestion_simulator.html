Transport Layer

Traffic Shaping
& Congestion Control

Observe how open-loop shaping and closed-loop congestion control manage bursts, queues, and loss under varying network conditions.

01 — Token Bucket

Controlled Burst

Tokens accrue at a fixed rate up to capacity. Packets depart only when tokens are available, allowing short bursts while enforcing a long-term average rate.

02 — Leaky Bucket

Constant Drain

Arrivals enter a finite buffer and exit at a constant rate. Excess traffic is discarded when the buffer is full, producing a smooth regulated output.

03 — TCP Reno

Reactive Window

Exponential growth in slow start, linear in congestion avoidance, and a multiplicative decrease on loss. Classic closed-loop response to congestion signals.
